## Challenger decisions

- Data-sublime field: declined on audience identification and product clarity. Keep its precision in numerical alignment; no strobing or barcode decoration.
- Warm consumer app: competitive in audience familiarity, weaker in distinctiveness after the rejected rounded-card design. Available as the familiar alternative; avoid another generic card wall.
- Botanical folio: competitive in product clarity. Keep fixed alignment across the five real question states, standard navigation and controls; omit botanical imagery and lifecycle metaphors.
- Crouwel grid: declined on audience identification and reading clarity. Keep consistent spacing and column discipline, without visible decorative grids.
- Character catalog: declined on audience identification and study clarity. Keep finite, named course units; no invented mascots or reward claims.
- Airline wallet: declined on study clarity and audience identification. Keep explicit completed/pending states, without ticket styling.
