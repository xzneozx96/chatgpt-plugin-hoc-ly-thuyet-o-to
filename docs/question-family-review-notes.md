## Group design

Coverage is meaningful without chapter-sized catchalls. Larger groups are bounded parameter matrices (eight rural-speed questions, licence/trailer groups), and narrower pairs support side-by-side teaching. Role and context comparisons recur across rules and culture chapters because the same action is asked from another role or purpose. relationship=topic indicates a pedagogical link rather than a claim that rules or correct answers are interchangeable. text_supported means the textual relationship is supported; it does not certify correctness or current legality of the bank.

## Image-dependent mapping limits

Identical stems such as “Biển báo này có ý nghĩa như thế nào?” and “Theo hướng mũi tên...” never establish duplication. Grouping uses explanations and option differences. Vehicle identities, numbered arrows, sign positions, lane colors, and trajectories must be resolved from actual images before a learner-facing comparison is accepted.
