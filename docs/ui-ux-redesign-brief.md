# Full experience redesign brief

6 October 2026. The owner rejected the current visual design and explicitly requested a complete redesign using Impeccable. Revision 3 is an anti-reference, not the visual system to preserve. This brief carries forward confirmed product requirements; it does not approve a replacement visual direction.

## Product facts already confirmed

- Vietnamese driving theory for licence B, experienced entirely through ChatGPT.
- Beginners and learners approaching their exam; daily study with deeper explanations.
- Original wording, answers, and images come from the supplied 600-question bank.
- Seven bank categories and the custom “Câu hỏi dễ nhầm lẫn” category, with 249 overlapping groups.
- Each category and group is a course unit. Show first-pass coverage, delayed learning, mistakes, unresolved confusion, and due reviews without mixing their meanings.
- Shared questions contribute once to total course coverage. Unit summaries reflect the same per-question records that ChatGPT retrieves.
- Complete due review first by default; honour an explicit request to study another unit or take a test.
- Learners choose a daily goal of 10, 12, 15, or a custom number of new questions. Review is additional.
- Explanations and points to remember have a visually distinct teaching area.
- Approved external knowledge and timestamped YouTube links support teaching. Unavailable sources must not be invented.
- Mock tests contain 30 questions, last 20 minutes, require 27 correct, and fail on any incorrect or unanswered critical question. Library data is still pending.
- The local prototype exists for design review. Production identity, durable progress, MCP integration, and host validation are separate implementation work.

## What the replacement must resolve

1. Make the course structure and next useful study action immediately understandable.
2. Give category and group progress a real place in the experience, including the five-question speed group example.
3. Make teaching and comparing conditions the central activity, with question navigation subordinate to it.
4. Avoid burying the course, review, and test entry points in vaguely labelled menus.
5. Establish a coherent visual language that differs materially from the rejected generic outlined card and form layout.
6. Keep the number of simultaneous decisions small and make return paths predictable.
7. Work inside a ChatGPT widget at desktop and narrow mobile widths.
8. Present real bank content and clearly identified sample learner history in design examples.

## Screens required to judge a direction

- Returning learner with category progress, due review, and the next action.
- “Câu hỏi dễ nhầm lẫn” discovery and an individual five-question unit.
- Original question, submitted feedback, and explanation/remembering section.
- Course progress and today's review queue using the same example learner records.
- Timed test with visible timer, navigation, and submission.

## Confirmed direction

The owner chose course overview plus Continue today and compared three working designs. On 6 October 2026 they accepted recommended direction B, Đường học. The accepted direction uses a blue course index, top navigation and a separate daily-action column, with the daily action first on phones. See ui-ux-direction-b-contract.md for the build contract.
